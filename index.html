Privacy Policy

App: JumpForge (com.jumpforge.app) · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the JumpForge mobile app ("the app"), which estimates jump and sprint metrics from video on your device and optionally records outdoor GNSS speed tracks (JumpForge Plus). We respect user privacy. This policy clearly explains what data the app processes — and what it does not.

What data the app processes

JumpForge analyzes videos and GNSS tracks on your device. There are no JumpForge accounts and no JumpForge server: we do not receive your videos, pose data, location, athlete profiles, or results. The app contains no advertising and no analytics.

Google components built into the app handle purchases and location fixes. See Google components below.

On-device processing includes:

  • Camera clips you record and videos you import (analysis and review). Imported videos are copied into app storage; your original file is not changed. The clip in app storage is deleted when you leave the results or load another clip.
  • Pose landmarks derived from video (event detection and skeleton overlay). Held in memory during analysis and not saved.
  • Location / GNSS fixes for the optional outdoor Speed track protocol (Plus) while you record a track. Coordinates are used in memory to estimate speed, distance, and acceleration and are not saved. If you save the track, History keeps time, distance, speed, and acceleration values only.
  • Athlete profiles (name, sex, height) in a local file.
  • Saved session metrics (History) in a local file, including Speed track rows you save.
  • JumpForge Plus purchase entitlement via Google Play Billing.

The app does not include:

  • JumpForge user accounts or sign-in
  • advertising or ad tracking
  • analytics or tracking services
  • background location tracking
  • upload of videos, analysis results, or GNSS tracks to developer servers

Permissions

  • Camera — only to record training clips you choose to capture. Not required if you only import videos or use Speed track.
  • Location (precise and approximate) — only for the optional outdoor Speed track protocol (Plus). Android offers both options in one prompt; Speed track needs precise location. Requested when you open Speed track and used in the foreground while you record a track. Not used for advertising or background tracking.
  • Internet and network state — declared for Google Play Billing. JumpForge's own code makes no network requests and does not upload videos, analysis results, or GNSS tracks.
  • Other permissions added by the Google libraries (Play Billing, wake lock) do not give access to personal data.
  • No microphone permission is requested for recording.

Google components

  • Google Play Billing — handles the optional JumpForge Plus purchase. See "JumpForge Plus" below.
  • Google Play services location — Speed track gets location fixes from Android's fused location provider, which is part of Google Play services on your device. Before a track, Android may show a system dialog asking you to turn on device location or location accuracy. Those settings, and any location data Google collects through them, are controlled by you in Android settings and governed by Google's privacy policy. JumpForge does not receive that data.
  • MediaPipe (pose analysis) — pose detection runs on your device with Google's MediaPipe library. Videos, images, and pose landmarks are not sent to Google. The usage-diagnostics reporting built into the Google libraries is switched off in JumpForge.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Cloud backup

Android cloud backup of captures, imports, and the local History file is disabled in the app. Your training videos, GNSS History rows, and History file are intended to remain on the device unless you deliberately share an export.

JumpForge Plus (in-app purchase)

Plus is a one-time unlock sold through Google Play. Payment and purchase records are processed by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy. JumpForge receives an entitlement signal from Play Billing on the device; we do not receive your payment card details.

Sharing

JumpForge does not sell personal data and does not send it to us or to advertisers. If you use History Export (Plus), you choose where to send the JSON backup (email, Files, etc.). That share is under your control. The file contains athlete profiles and saved History rows, including Speed track rows you saved. A copy of each exported file stays in the app's cache until you clear app storage or cache, or uninstall.

Children's privacy

JumpForge is a general sports training tool. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not enter personal data of children if that would conflict with local law.

Data retention and deletion

Videos are deleted when you leave the results or load another clip. If the app is closed in the middle of an analysis, a clip can remain in app storage until you clear app storage or uninstall. Athlete profiles and History stay on your device until you delete sessions/athletes in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall. Uninstalling removes local app data on typical Android devices.
