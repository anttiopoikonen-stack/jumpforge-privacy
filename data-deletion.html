Data deletion request — JumpForge

App: JumpForge · Developer contact: antti.o.poikonen@gmail.com · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains how to delete data associated with the JumpForge Android app (package com.jumpforge.app), as shown on the JumpForge Google Play listing.

What JumpForge stores

JumpForge does not operate a JumpForge account system and does not upload your training videos or History to JumpForge servers. Training data stays on your device unless you deliberately export or share it.

  • On-device: recorded/imported videos, pose analysis cache, athlete profiles (name, sex, height), and History session metrics.
  • Google Play: if you buy JumpForge Plus, purchase records are handled by Google Play under Google's terms (not stored on a JumpForge server).

How to delete your data

Do one of the following:

  • In the app: delete individual History sessions and/or athlete profiles you no longer want.
  • Clear app storage: on your Android device open Settings → Apps → JumpForge → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. This removes local videos, History, and athlete profiles stored by JumpForge.
  • Uninstall JumpForge: uninstalling removes JumpForge app data from typical Android devices.

You can also email antti.o.poikonen@gmail.com with subject “JumpForge data deletion” if you need help. Because we do not hold your training data on JumpForge servers, deletion is completed on the device using the steps above.

What is deleted vs retained

  • Deleted by clear data / uninstall: local videos under JumpForge storage, local History, athlete profiles, and local Plus entitlement flags on that device.
  • Not deleted by uninstalling JumpForge alone: copies you previously exported/shared yourself (email, Files, cloud folders you chose). Delete those where you saved them.
  • Google Play purchases: JumpForge Plus payment and purchase history remain with Google according to Google's retention practices. Manage or review purchases in your Google account / Google Play. JumpForge cannot erase Google's purchase records.

Retention period

On-device JumpForge data is kept only while it remains on your device — there is no JumpForge cloud retention period. It is removed when you delete it in-app, clear app storage, or uninstall, as described above. Google Play may retain purchase-related records for as long as Google requires for payments, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.
